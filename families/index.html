A Want List should do more than remember. It connects what families are hoping to buy with offers participating businesses choose to make available. With permission-based, aggregated demand signals, merchants can see what customers are looking for and make better inventory decisions.

Want Lists connect household interest with retailer-approved offers and aggregated demand. Budget meal planning and Fridge Rescue help turn what is already at home into dinner, with favorite store alerts, household savings, dietary preferences, and claim, pay and pickup in one flow.
