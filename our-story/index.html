Our Story

I saw the problem from both sides.
So I decided to build the connection.

Nancy Hassan
Founder, Save4Good. Woman-founded. Mom of three.

I am Nancy Hassan, a mom of three and the founder of Save4Good. Like so many parents, I know what it means to plan around a family budget, a busy day and the things that cannot wait.

One evening, after a long day at work, I bought my daughter's baby formula for $70. The next day, I saw the exact same formula on a final-clearance shelf for $10. I did not buy it because I already had what she needed. But I kept thinking about another parent who might need it and never know it was there.

While volunteering, I watched families open donated grocery boxes containing things they could not use. I thought about community organizations and senior homes with real wishlists and dietary needs that rarely reached the businesses able to respond.

I have also spent years leading hospitality operations and managing teams. I know how much time, labor and financial pressure businesses carry. Even good inventory and useful resources can be difficult to move when managers are already stretched.

The needs were real. The value was real. The connection was missing.

I thought about the hot meals a grocery kitchen may not sell, and the employee on a warehouse shift who wishes a hot meal were within reach. The need is real. The value is real. The connection is missing.

That is why I am building Save4Good: connected infrastructure that helps businesses act on demand, unlock opportunities and document outcomes while giving people more access and more choice.

And as Save4Good grows, I hope it opens meaningful doors for other women and mothers. There is extraordinary talent behind ordinary family routines, and I want more of that potential to have room to grow.

More revenue for businesses. More possibilities for people. More good for our planet.
Nancy Hassan, Founder, Save4Good
