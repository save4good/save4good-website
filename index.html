More than a listing. A partnership that grows your business.

Save4Good is building a partnership model around your business, not just a page for your products. When you request a partnership, this is what we work toward with you, agreed in writing before any listing goes live.

📅 Monthly Performance Meeting

Every month, we sit down with you and review what actually happened: recovered revenue, demand, what sold, what did not, and what to change next month. No guessing, no vague report.

📈 Performance Development Plan

A written plan for your business, with clear goals, product categories, pricing approach, buyer channels, and milestones. Reviewed and updated at each monthly meeting, so growth is planned, not accidental.

📄 Monthly Proof of Second Life

One clear monthly page for your business, based only on store-confirmed sales — recovered revenue, items moved, and new buyers reached. Pickup alone is never counted as a sale.

🔍 Demand Intelligence Report

Aggregated, anonymised demand from real want lists near you, so you can see what people are actually asking for before you mark down, move, or clear stock.

🎓 Free Training for Your Team

Free training for your named store numbers person, with a simple workflow designed to avoid extra work for your staff. Listing, picking, and handoff times are measured in a pilot, not promised in advance.

🤝 Private B2B Channel

Move larger quantities through targeted, private buyer relationships, without public discounting, when a public listing is not the right channel.

Our founder's story

“It started with a simple moment at the grocery store.”

As a mother of three, I bought my daughter’s formula for $70. The next day, I discovered the same formula for $10, but I did not purchase it again. The point was not retailer price competition. It was limited visibility into existing merchant-managed opportunities, and demand that was not connected to what was available. I hope Save4Good creates meaningful opportunities for other women and mothers as it grows.

— Nancy Hassan, Founder of Save4Good. Woman-founded and inspired by a mom of three.
