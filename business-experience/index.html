How was this estimated?

Margin per unit = chosen price − your $1.85 margin floor. Estimated weekly margin = margin per unit × the illustrative estimated units per week for that price in the chart above (for example, $2.49 − $1.85 = $0.64 per unit; $0.64 × 38 est. units/week ≈ $24.32/week above the floor). Chart volumes are example figures in this example, drawn to illustrate the concept from stock on hand, days on shelf, and comparable nearby prices, they are not live demand data or a forecast, and no result is guaranteed.
